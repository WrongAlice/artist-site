02 / BEHIND THE WORK

A little about

Hi, I’m Nicole. This is a place for the things I make, collect, and dream up. I’m drawn to texture, odd details, and pieces with a life of their own.

I like to craft whimsical, useable objects. I am inspired by nature and magic. I work from my home in a perfectly desolate town in N.E. Pennsylvania.
