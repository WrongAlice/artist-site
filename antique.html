02 / A NOTE ON COLLECTING

discovering
the softness of time

I’m drawn to the things that carry traces of another life: a faded pattern, an unexpected shape, a detail someone took the time to make by hand.

This notebook is a place to follow that curiosity, gather inspiration, and share what catches my eye.
